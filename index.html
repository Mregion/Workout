Exercise Warm-Up Sets Hard Sets Reps Rest Between Hard Sets
Warm-Up Sets: warm-up sets prepare the movement and progressively increase the load. They are NOT hard sets and should not be taken close to failure.

Hard Sets: only working sets performed at the prescribed training intensity count as hard sets.

Rest: the rest time shown in the table is the recommended rest BETWEEN HARD SETS. Warm-up sets generally require less rest.

Progression: stay inside the prescribed rep range. When all hard sets reach the top of the range with clean technique, increase the weight.
